# Greek flavor library

This shelf is for engagement and explicit adaptation comparisons. Every item is modern reception, not evidence for ancient belief or a replacement for the core library. The notes below are intentionally brief and original; they do not reproduce protected text.

## Comparison record template

When adding a new flavor item, record:

- stable ID, title, creator, release date, and medium;
- the specific mythic figures or episodes it uses;
- the official creator/publisher source used to verify the adaptation metadata;
- relevant core-library source IDs;
- at least one concrete difference, attributed to the adaptation;
- content notes where helpful;
- no copied lyrics, dialogue, passages, or substantial plot prose.

Do not label an adaptation “inaccurate” as a blanket judgment. Prefer specific, checkable comparisons such as “this version combines two episodes,” “this character’s role is newly invented,” or “this work follows Euripides’ version rather than the Homeric one.”
